One thing to flag: you specified model `claude-sonnet-5-5`, which I used literally — but the known current model ID is `claude-sonnet-5` (no trailing `-5`). If `claude-sonnet-5-5` isn't a real model on your account, the SDK call will 404; the fix is just changing the `MODEL` constant at the top of `solution.mjs`.
